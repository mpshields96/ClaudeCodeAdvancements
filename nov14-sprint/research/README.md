# Research source (code and protocols only)

Two self-contained research folders, published as source only. No price rows, vendor files, downloaded pages, fill logs, forecasts, results or reports are included. Each folder keeps its raw inputs and outputs in a local `private/` folder that its `.gitignore` excludes.

| Folder | What it checks |
|---|---|
| `gold_history/` | Falsification test of a Reddit-described intraday gold swing-break basket on public XAU/USD minute bid candles, frozen protocol in `PREREGISTRATION.md`. |
| `nfl_rb_receiving_check/` | Registered, week-ordered forecast check for running-back receiving yards over 22.5 on public nflverse tables, frozen protocol in `protocol.md`. |

Research only. Nothing here places, sizes or recommends a trade or wager. Stdlib Python 3.9 (`/usr/bin/python3 -I`).
