# Independent gold research protocol

Frozen 2026-11-15 at 16:43 UTC, before acquiring or reading gold price rows. This is a falsification test of specified interpretations of the Reddit post. The original article leaves its final parameters and source series private. This research does not alter any bot.

**Sample.** Request the public HistData Generic ASCII XAU/USD M1 bid candles for calendar years 2021, 2022 and 2023. The 2021–22 estimates give historical context, and 2023 alone is the prespecified final evaluation. If some of these files are unavailable, report their absence and examine available ones without replacing them based on performance. If this publisher cannot supply any, try official Dukascopy historical XAU/USD export for the same years. If vendor limits preclude full years, use the first available complete calendar month in each year, in calendar order. Never substitute another traded instrument. Fixed-source EST (UTC−05:00 all year) will be converted to New York civil time. For evaluation, schedule weekdays 08:00–15:55 New York. Run only sessions with each of the 476 required minute timestamps available in order, excluding duplicates, invalid OHLC and nonfinite values. Log exclusions and all qualifying weekdays including no-trade days. Report exclusion risk. No overnight holdings, optimization, warm-up across days or daily quota for trades.

**Signal.** Aggregate the 08:00 through 15:54 minute candles to consecutive five-minute bid bars. A pivot high or low must be strictly above or below the six preceding and six following highs or lows. At five-minute bar close t only pivots centered at t−6 have just become knowable. Use the most recent eligible pivot on each side. A close strictly beyond it generates a directional break, once per pivot. All price decisions use only completed bars. At 15:55 flatten on the first quoted bid open, with modeled execution costs. Do not enter at or after 15:55.

**Trading.** All modes keep USD cash plus one net XAU position. Each entry buys at the displayed bid open plus the modeled full spread and slip, or sells at bid open minus slip. Full-spread cases are USD 0.00, 0.30, 0.60 and 1.00 per oz, each with USD 0.00, 0.05, 0.10 and 0.25 adverse slippage per oz per side respectively. Base case is 0.30/0.05, total 0.40 per ounce round trip. These synthetic cost inputs include no documented broker commission, subscription or financing and can understate costs. The instrument is a spot quote proxy. Close a basket when the one-minute closing liquidation mark reaches +$3 or −$6 after spread and incurred slippage. Fill on the NEXT minute open, with exit costs, so amounts may overshoot. Exit takes priority over pending entries. A swing-break order enters on the first minute of the next five-minute bar. No additional entry can interrupt an already queued exit. Reset after exit. A signal on a bar that triggers an exit is skipped.

**Predeclared modes.** Cash. Single 1 oz ignoring all signals until flat. Fixed basket enters up to three legs of 1/1/1 oz, including repeat and reverse on distinct confirmed pivots. Escalating basket enters 1/2/4 oz subject to the same three-leg cap. A second escalating variant accepts alternating directions only. Every mode has the same basket cash thresholds and scheduled liquidation. The maximum signed exposure differs, and the two baskets have conceptually higher gross exposures. Use a $10,000 reference balance (no balance or margin calls modeled). No sizing by wealth. Primary comparison is final-year mean daily net dollars after the base assumed costs vs cash, with the $3/day aspiration independently shown. No choice based on strongest year, mode, cost or metric. Report sample, basket round-trips, mean/median/worst day, positive-day share, total, intraday marked drawdown and weekly-block resampling intervals on daily mean. Compare cost scenarios and opening/day coverage. Examine the spread in the provider's tick data on the first available full January weekday of final year if independently available; this is a limited sanity check, not a retrospective replacement of the base case.
