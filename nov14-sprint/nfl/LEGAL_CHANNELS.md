## 2. Parish limits

Official source: the LGCB annual report (OFFICIAL, URL above, sports wagering section). It says 55 of 64 parishes voted in favor in the 2020 local-option referendum, and names the parishes that voted NO:

**Caldwell, Catahoula, Franklin, Jackson, LaSalle, Sabine, Union, West Carroll, Winn.**

The same report says a person must be physically located in a parish that approved the proposition, so mobile wagering is tied to location. Check which parish you are in before relying on any app.

- Mechanism (OFFICIAL): Act 215 (SB 130), 2020 Regular Session, digest at https://legis.la.gov/legis/ViewDocument.aspx?d=1180162. It set a parish referendum on 11/3/2020. A "for" majority permits sports wagering in the parish once the state licensing law takes effect, and an "against" majority prohibits it.
- Secretary of State parish results: NOT retrieved. The voterportal page returned an unrendered script template. UNVERIFIED against the SOS.
- SECONDARY cross-checks: Jackson Parish Journal (https://jacksonparishjournal.com/2020/11/06/jackson-parish-votes-against-sports-wagering-option/) reports Jackson voted 3,580 no to 3,483 yes. Action Network (https://www.actionnetwork.com/legal-online-sports-betting/which-louisiana-parishes-will-not-allow-sports-betting, last updated 6/26/2024) said "56 of 64" and my fetch listed only 8 parishes (Jackson missing). I follow the LGCB's 9.
- Later status changes: I found no official record of any parish changing status since 2020. UNVERIFIED. A Legal Sports Report piece on a possible re-vote bill returned 403 and was not read.

## 3. EXCLUDED products

**Event-contract and prediction-market products are EXCLUDED across brands regardless of what any app offers.** This covers Kalshi, Polymarket (including Polymarket US), Robinhood prediction markets, Crypto.com, Coinbase, Gemini, Underdog, Novig, ForecastEX, ProphetX, and any similar "contract", "swap", "market" or "financial instrument" tied to a sports outcome. If a licensed brand's app also shows an event-contract section, that section is excluded too (this is a rule for this project, not a retrieved fact about any specific app).

Correction to the brief: the AG did not file first. Kalshi sued the state pre-emptively (below). The AG's own state suit and restraining order came on 11/12/2026, and I only found secondary reports of it.

Evidence, strongest first:
1. OFFICIAL, LGCB Advisory Notice on Prediction Markets, 12/5/2025, ref. LGCB-8716-25, signed by Chairman Christopher B. Hebert (https://lgcb.dps.louisiana.gov/media/fgfaspij/20251205-prediction-market-notice.pdf; news item https://lgcb.dps.louisiana.gov/news/louisiana-gaming-control-board-issues-advisory-notice-on-prediction-markets-to-louisiana-sports-wagering-operators/). It says sports and other event contracts are illegal gambling "whether the contract is listed on an exchange regulated by the [CFTC] or elsewhere", and may be offered only by a Board-licensed or permitted operator. It names no companies. Image PDF, read visually.
2. Court filing, primary but hosted on a secondary site: KalshiEX LLC v. Murrill et al., W.D. La., 1:26-cv-04476, Doc 1, filed 10/23/2026 (https://www.sportsbettingdime.com/app/uploads/2026/10/gov.uscourts.lawd_.224598.1.0.pdf). It is Kalshi's allegations, not findings. It alleges AG Murrill called prediction markets gambling and joined amicus briefs against Kalshi, and that enforcement is imminent. This is NOT the AG's own filing.
3. SECONDARY, AG state suit and TRO, 11/12/2026, 21st JDC, Tangipahoa Parish, Judge Jeffrey Johnson:
   - CDC Gaming (11/13): ten defendants, Kalshi, Polymarket, Robinhood, Crypto.com, Coinbase, Novig, Underdog, Gemini, ForecastEX, ProphetX. The TRO bars event contracts without Louisiana licenses, requires geofencing, runs 10 days, with a hearing set for 11/30. https://cdcgaming.com/louisiana-secures-restraining-order-against-prediction-markets-companies/
   - Sports Betting Dime (11/12): names Kalshi, Polymarket, Robinhood, Crypto.com, Underdog, Novig, ProphetX plus "several other". https://www.sportsbettingdime.com/news/industry/louisiana-attorney-general-files-sweeping-prediction-market-lawsuit/
   - WAFB (11/12): names Kalshi, Polymarket, Robinhood, Coinbase, Gemini. https://www.wafb.com/2026/11/12/louisiana-suing-kalshi-polymarket-other-online-prediction-markets/
   - The Advocate (11/14): TRO applies only to the prediction-market parts of Robinhood and Coinbase. It does not state a duration or hearing date. https://www.theadvocate.com/baton_rouge/news/a-judge-ordered-kalshi-polymarket-other-prediction-markets-to-halt-in-louisiana-what-s-next/article_aae9d12d-91ca-54b0-ab31-c3c3ecf9407d.html
   - Outlets differ on the defendant list. The 10-day term and 11/30 hearing rest on CDC Gaming alone.
4. NOT FOUND: the AG's own press release or petition. The AG news page (https://www.ag.louisiana.gov/news) listed releases only through 11/12/2026, none on prediction markets. The AG's X post is cited only via SBD (https://x.com/AGLizMurrill/status/2120937238565470720) and was not fetched.
5. UNVERIFIED: Polymarket's own suit, QCX LLC v. Murrill, E.D. La., 2:26-cv-02482, filed 10/27/2026. Search-result summary only, no page retrieved. New criminal and enforcement laws effective 8/1/2026 (La. R.S. 14:90, 14:90.1, 14:90.3, 27:19.1, 27:19.2, 27:604) also come from search results, not a retrieved statute.

Not a licensed channel:
- Offshore and unlicensed books and sweepstakes casinos: the LGCB "List of Operators Issued Cease-and-Desist Letters" (OFFICIAL, https://lgcb.dps.louisiana.gov/illegal-operators/) lists about 41 entries. Examples: Bovada (Harp Media), BetUS, BetNow, BetAnySports, BookMaker, Youwager, BetWhale, Fliff, VGW Holdings, Wow Vegas, Chanced Casino, Smiles Casino. My read of the page may miss rows. It does not mention prediction markets, Kalshi or fantasy. The annual report (OFFICIAL) says the Board sent over 40 cease-and-desist letters in FY 2024-25 to offshore wagering platforms and online sweepstakes operators.
- Daily fantasy and pick'em: the only licensed fantasy operators are DraftKings and FanDuel (section 1). No official Louisiana source I retrieved addresses other fantasy or pick'em apps; they are simply not on the licensed list.
- LGCB press release 11/12/2026 for Illegal Gambling Awareness Week (OFFICIAL, https://lgcb.dps.louisiana.gov/media/emqeqjk0/20261112-press-release.pdf) tells residents to verify an operator is authorized. It does not mention prediction markets.
