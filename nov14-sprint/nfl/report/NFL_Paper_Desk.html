No Demonstrated Edge

Four simple betting rules, tested once on 3 seasons they had never seen, at the standard -110 price. What you can use this weekend is a paper ledger for your own takes and passes. It bets nothing.

The answer

None of the four rules passed the test. The test was set in writing before any result was seen: a rule passes only if its whole 95% interval sits above the 52.38% win rate needed to break even at -110. The samples are small, so this fails to show an edge. It does not prove there is none.

The best-looking rule, home underdogs getting 7 or more points, went 38-23-2 (62.3%). On only 63 bets its interval runs from 49.7% to 73.4%, which still includes break-even. It is a lead to keep watching on paper, not a reason to bet.

The test

Why home underdogs, 7+ points has not shown an edge

It was the best of four rules, on 63 bets (9-8-2 in 2023, 11-6-0 in 2024, 18-9-0 in 2025). Its 95% interval starts at 49.7%, below the 52.38% break-even; the stricter interval that allows for four rules starts at 46.3%. A break-even rule would show a record this good about 7.7% of the time alone, and about 31% once four rules are allowed for. Those seasons are now spent: only new games can judge the rule, which is the paper ledger's job.

Baselines, held-out at -110: always home -4.1%, underdog -7.4%, under -5.8%. An AI model that may remember 2023 to 2025 chose the rule families, so this test is not fully blind; only the forward ledger is clean.

Your own picks

The data covers team markets only: spread, total and moneyline. It has no player props. Your way of choosing, watching the game and judging player roles, goes in as a personal entry: the price you were actually offered, where and when you saw it, take or pass, and your reason. A prop at 54.5 and the same prop at 60.5 are saved as two different contracts. A closing price counts only for the identical contract, read after your pick and before kickoff. Any other quote is kept, labeled and left out of that comparison.

Use it

How to use it, and where the limits are

Opening flow

# From the ClaudeCodeAdvancements folder. System python3 only, nothing to install. # 1. See the slate and where each moneyline sits against your +150 to -170 range python3 -I nov14-sprint/nfl/paper_ledger.py slate # 2. Log the frozen rules' takes and passes (done for week 10; a repeat adds nothing) python3 -I nov14-sprint/nfl/paper_ledger.py generate # 3. Your own take at the price you were offered. --quote-ts is the UTC time you read # the price; the date command below fills in the time right now. python3 -I nov14-sprint/nfl/paper_ledger.py add --game-id 2026_10_PIT_CIN \ --rule MANUAL-v1 --market prop --selection "Player Name receiving yards" \ --side over --line 54.5 --price -115 --action take --reason "role grew" \ --source "operator name" --quote-ts "$(date -u +%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ)" # 4. Your own pass on another pick: the same flags with --action pass. Stake is 0. # 5. The same player relisted at 60.5 is a separate contract: change --line to 60.5. # Sending an identical contract again is refused as DUPLICATE_PICK. # 6. Near kickoff: the price for the identical contract. PICK_ID is printed by step 3. python3 -I nov14-sprint/nfl/paper_ledger.py close --pick-id PICK_ID --line 54.5 \ --price -125 --source "operator name" --quote-ts "$(date -u +%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ)" # 7. After the games: team markets settle from a fresh download, props by hand. # NEW_GAMES_CSV is the new file; RETRIEVED_UTC is when it was downloaded. python3 -I nov14-sprint/nfl/paper_ledger.py settle --results-file NEW_GAMES_CSV \ --results-ts RETRIEVED_UTC python3 -I nov14-sprint/nfl/paper_ledger.py settle-manual --pick-id PICK_ID \ --outcome win --result-source "box score, site name" python3 -I nov14-sprint/nfl/paper_ledger.py status

The tool is nov14-sprint/nfl/paper_ledger.py and its records are in nov14-sprint/nfl/ledger/. To practise without touching the real ledger, put --ledger-dir and an empty folder of your choice straight after paper_ledger.py. Every command prints "PAPER ONLY". The ledger refuses a game that has kicked off, a missing or stale line, a quote time later than the clock, a stake above 1 unit, and a repeat of the same contract. It stops taking new paper takes after a 10 unit drawdown. Its self-test is nov14-sprint/nfl/tests/fixtures/selftest_paper_ledger.py.

Limits

  • No rule has a demonstrated edge. Nothing here supports a real bet.
  • Team markets only. Props are personal entries with prices you type in.
  • The file's line is not verified as a closing line, and its book is not named.
  • The 2023 to 2025 seasons are spent. Only new games can test a rule now.
  • Team markets settle from a fresh download of the same file. Props settle by hand.
  • Closing prices are recorded and labeled. No closing-line value is computed yet.
