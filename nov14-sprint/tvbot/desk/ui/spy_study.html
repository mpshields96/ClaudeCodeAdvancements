    Source, timing and cost assumptions

    FirstRate provided all 3,900 expected regular-session SPY minutes from November 2–13, 2026. The first six sessions supplied history. The remaining four provided 24 decisions with a following bar in the same session. The last bar of each day lasted 30 minutes. The November 11 two-share cases form a retrospective sizing comparison.

    Signals use the shipped frozen shadow rule. A rising or falling eight-bar exponential moving average must agree with the close, six-bar volatility must be within 10–150 basis points, and the estimated one-bar drift must clear the cost hurdle. An entry is modeled one minute after the decision and closed at the end of the following slot. Each side costs one basis point of half-spread, two of slippage, and an assumed $1 commission. Individual outcomes are rounded to the nearest $0.25. The PDF uses unrounded totals. These are historical trade bars. The authenticated desk requests free IEX data separately.
